About kimbap

Kimbap, also known as gimbap is a popular korean dish with rice and various fillings

Kimbap makes a great quick snack,side dish, or even and entire meal.

A photo of kimbap!

Kimbap ingredients:

  • Sheets of seaweed
  • Jasmin rice -2 cups-
  • Beef bulgolgi -1.5 lb-
  • Carrots -1-
  • Spinach -1-2 cups-
  • Eggs -2-
  • Sesame seeds
  • Sesame seed oil

Kimbap recipe

  1. Cook the rice according the the instructions on your package
  2. Cook the beef bulgogi, heat a little oil in a pan over medium heat, stirring ocasionally until fully cooked
  3. Prepare the carrots, peel the carrots and cut them into thin match sticks, saute them in a pan with a little oil for about 3 minutes
  4. Cook the spinach in boiling water for about 60 seconds, then drain and cool them. Add in a little sesame seed oil for flavor
  5. Make the egg strips, heat an oiled pan over medium low heat and cook the eggs into a thin omelet, then let cool and slice into long thin strips.
  6. Lay out the seaweed and rice, place a seaweed sheet shiny side down on a rolling mat or cutting board. Spread a thin and even layer of rice over thhe seaweed.
  7. Place a little beef bulgogi,carrot,spinach,and egg in horizontal lines across the rice. Avoid over filling!
  8. Lift the edges closets to you and roll the seaweed tightly over the fillings. Roll gently pressing to keep the fillings inside.
  9. Light pour some sesame oil and top with sesame seeds. Use a sharp knife to cut eat roll into slices. And enjoy!
